About OzShine

Pride in every polish, wash and finishing pass.

For more than 15 years, OzShine has treated vehicle care as more than routine maintenance. The work is about preserving presence, protecting paintwork and making every car feel looked after properly.

Our Approach

A better way to care for the vehicles people rely on every day.

OzShine specialises in meticulous hand washing and detailing that goes beyond a standard clean to protect your investment and sharpen the way your vehicle feels day to day.
